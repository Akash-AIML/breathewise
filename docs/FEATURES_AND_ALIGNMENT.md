# BreatheWise — Problem Statement Alignment & Feature Guide

## 1. Core Features
- **Live Air Quality Dashboard:** Real-time CPCB/OpenAQ/Open-Meteo telemetry, pollutant breakdown (PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃), provenance, and freshness status.
- **Location Precision:** Browser geolocation with accuracy badge, OpenStreetMap Nominatim search proxy, popular Indian city stations, and persistent manual `lat, lon` override.
- **Household Profile & Daily Planner:** Tailored morning/afternoon/evening action cards for children, elderly, respiratory/cardiac sensitivities, and diurnal winter thermal inversion windows (6:00–9:30 AM, 1:00–4:00 PM, 6:30–10:30 PM).
- **Indoor Air Guide:** 5-ACH HEPA CADR sizing calculator (`Area_sqft × 1.16 m³/hr`), interactive Indoor Air Hack Impact Simulator (HEPA + draft sealing + kitchen exhaust), and houseplant myth clarification.
- **Sustainable Transit & Community Brief:** Direct Impact of Your Commute Choice calculator (ARAI/CEA emission factors vs. PM2.5 inhalation trade-offs) and copyable RWA/School Smog Action Brief.
- **24-Hour Trends, Comparison & Map:** Recharts trend chart with accessible data table, multi-station comparison, and keyboard-navigable India station map.
- **Multi-Turn AI Assistant:** Context-aware Q&A grounded in live station telemetry and deterministic policy actions.
